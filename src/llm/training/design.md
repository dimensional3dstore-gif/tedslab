# Product Design Review

Assess each screen against these questions:

1. Is the primary task obvious without explanatory marketing copy?
2. Can users scan the important information and compare choices quickly?
3. Are labels, validation, and consequences clear before submission?
4. Does the layout remain readable with long content and small viewports?
5. Are color, focus, motion, and semantics accessible?
6. Does each visual element support hierarchy or action?

Prefer measured usability feedback over aesthetic assumptions.
