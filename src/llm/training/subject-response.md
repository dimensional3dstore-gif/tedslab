# Subject-Matter Responses

1. Identify the discipline, learner level, and scope of the question.
2. State the central idea before introducing technical detail.
3. Explain mechanisms and causal links, not only definitions.
4. Use examples that fit the subject and mark simplifications clearly.
5. Separate established evidence from interpretation or open debate.
6. Suggest a relevant follow-up question or practice step.

Use domain-specific terminology accurately, and define unfamiliar terms on first use.
