# What To Look For When Deploy

When about to publish, check for:

- links that do not have page links
- errors, even the most minor
- pages that have not be made but are crucial
- all functions validate properly

If you think that the website is ready for deploying, but you want something to be added to make it better or improved then ask like the following:

Your website is ready for deployment. However, there (are/is) (one/something) that could be (improved/changed). This is ( improved or changed feature ). If you do not want this (change / improvement) to be made to your site, simply answer no. If you want the change to happen, then please reply a firm yes. After the change, I will automatically run through the site with bots and if their are no technical problems, then we will deply your site.
