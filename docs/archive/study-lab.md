## Study Lab

### Current design

- 3 subjects per day
- 4 sequential lesson stages per subject
- 60-minute lesson block
- Subject quiz after each subject
- End Of Lessons Quiz after all three subject quizzes
- Weekly mastery cycle with an 80% target
- Study Archive for completed days
